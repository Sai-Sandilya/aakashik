Return & Refund Policy

Refund Disclaimer Terms Privacy Shipping

Due to the nature of our wellness and consumable products, we do not accept returns or refunds once an order has been placed.

However, if you receive a damaged, defective, incorrect, or incomplete product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Please provide your order number and clear photographs or videos of the product and packaging for verification. After reviewing the issue, Aakashik Wellness may provide a replacement or refund, as applicable.

Non-Returnable Products

Returns and refunds will generally not be accepted for:

  • Opened or used products
  • Partially consumed products
  • Products damaged due to improper storage or handling
  • Products returned due to change of mind
  • Products where the customer simply does not like the taste, smell, texture, or individual experience

Nothing in this policy limits any rights available to consumers under applicable law.
